# Track 1 — Price comparison and AI shopping incumbents (new goods)

Output: `research/findings/01-competitors-new-price-comparison.md`

## Goal
Map every meaningful product that already helps a shopper find the best price on **new** goods, so we know what is commodity and what is still unsolved.

## Questions
1. **Australian price comparison.** Which services operate in Australia today? Check at least: Google Shopping, PriceSpy AU, PriceHipster, Getprice, MyShopping, Shopbot, StaticICE, Zyft, BuyWisely, Little Birdie, Booko, WhistleOut-style verticals, OzBargain, Finder, Canstar. For each: still alive? coverage (how many retailers, which categories), mobile app and its rating and review count, price history, alerts, business model, and whether it shows any used listings.
2. **Global leaders.** Idealo, PriceRunner/Klarna, Kelkoo, PriceGrabber, Shopzilla, ShopSavvy, Camelcamelcamel, Keepa, Honey, Capital One Shopping, Rakuten. What do they do well, what are their documented weaknesses, and are any present or expanding in Australia?
3. **AI shopping assistants.** ChatGPT shopping and agentic checkout, Perplexity shopping, Google AI Mode / Gemini shopping and its agentic features, Amazon Rufus and "Buy for Me", Microsoft Copilot shopping, Klarna AI, and startups (for example Phia, Daydream, Claros, Wizard, Dupe.com). Which are available to Australian users now? Do any cover second-hand listings or give a buy/wait/skip verdict? This is the largest competitive threat — be specific about what they can and cannot do today.
4. **Coverage test.** For the concrete case "fishing rod and reel combo" (pick a specific, real mid-range model sold at BCF), which of the above actually return useful Australian results? Report what you observe, and note where you could not test directly.
5. **Graveyard.** Which comparison products shut down or pivoted in the last ten years, in Australia and globally, and what reasons were given? Little Birdie is one to check.
6. **What is commodity versus unsolved.** From the evidence, list the features that every incumbent has, and the features none or almost none have.
